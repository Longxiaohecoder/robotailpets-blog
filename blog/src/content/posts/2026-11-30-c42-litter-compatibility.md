If you are buying litter for a Robotail UCAT C42, check two things on the bag: **whether it clumps and how long the grains are**. The [UCAT C42 manual](https://robotailpets.com/pages/c42m) lists clumping litter with grains shorter than 15 mm (about 0.6 in). When you refill, stop below the MAX line shown in the [illustrated UCAT C42 manual](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/I/B1uUA5iatfL.pdf). The grain limit tells you what to buy. The fill line tells you how much to add.

## Check the bag before buying

Start with your cat’s current litter. If the packaging or manufacturer lists it as clumping and confirms the grain length, you can check it against the C42 specification before buying anything new. If the package gives a range, check its upper end. If it gives no dimensions, ask the litter maker or check its technical information. Words such as “fine” or “automatic litter box compatible” do not tell you how long the grains are.

The manual suggests a tofu-and-cassava blend. That is one example offered by the manufacturer. A bag labeled tofu, cassava or clay still needs to meet the clumping and grain-length criteria. If you are considering a non-clumping product or a litter with an unclear specification, give Robotail support the exact litter name and the model number on your unit before filling the drum.

This guide applies to the **UCAT C42**. If you arrived from the [C42 product page](https://robotailpets.com/products/c42) or bought through a marketplace, check the label on your machine and use the manual for that model.

## Use the MAX line when refilling

![Robotail UCAT C42 manual illustration pointing to the maximum litter fill level inside the drum](/images/products/c42-manual-max-fill.webp)

*Robotail UCAT C42 manual, replacing litter, step 6. The callout marks the upper fill limit.*

With the drum stationary, look inside the entrance for the mark shown in the diagram. Add litter a scoop at a time and check the level across the bed. Leave it below the mark. If litter is piled in one spot, check the level once it is evenly spread before deciding whether to add another scoop. Use the flatten function as described in the manual, and keep hands clear while the drum moves.

If you are replacing all the litter, follow the separate emptying instructions first. The manual uses the removable litter-cleaning board for emptying the drum. Take it out as directed once the emptying cycle has finished before putting new litter in. Leaving it installed can send usable litter into the waste bin.

## Change one thing at a time

If your cat already uses a compatible litter, keeping that familiar litter when setting up the C42 gives you one fewer change to manage. Keep a familiar tray available while your cat gets used to the new box, and let your cat approach on their own. If you are checking how a new litter behaves, note the exact variety and check what ends up in the waste bin before changing anything else. If clean litter keeps getting discarded, recheck the grain length, the fill level and whether the emptying board was removed. If those checks do not explain it, contact Robotail support with the model and litter name.
