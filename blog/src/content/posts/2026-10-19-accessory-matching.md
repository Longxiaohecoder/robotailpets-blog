Ordering an accessory for your cat’s litter box should feel straightforward. Yet a familiar product photo or a slightly different model name can leave you wondering whether you have found the right part. A few checks before checkout can help you make a clearer choice.

Start with two questions: What exact model do you own, and what job should the accessory perform? Together, those answers are more useful than matching colors, shapes, or a product name that sounds close.

## Start with your own model label

Photograph the rating or model label on your unit so you can read the identifier while shopping. Crop out private information before sharing the photo. Keep the model text legible, including any letters or numbers after the main model name.

You may encounter different identifiers across sales channels. The [Amazon listing for B0G648Z6YL](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0G648Z6YL) identifies its model as **C32RO**, while the [listing for B0H2YPTF68](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0H2YPTF68) identifies **C42C**. These are listing identities; they do not establish accessory fit.

If a suffix on your label differs from the accessory description, ask Robotail support to confirm the match. A suffix alone is not enough to conclude either compatibility or incompatibility.

## Match waste bags to the stated models

The official [Robotail garbage-bag listing](https://robotailpets.com/products/garbage-bags) describes drawstring bags specifically for **C32 and C42**, supplied as a **60-pack: three rolls of 20 bags**.

![Official Robotail product image of C32 and C42 waste bags.](/images/products/c32-c42-bags.jpg)

When reviewing your order, check that you have saved this intended bag listing and that its stated models match the information you have confirmed for your unit. If your label includes an unclear suffix, include that detail in your support question.

Pack quantity tells you what is included in the order. It does not tell you how long the pack will last in your household.

## Check what “filter” means here

The official [C42 small-hole filter listing](https://robotailpets.com/products/c42-holefilter-accessory) describes a **C42-specific part for separating clumps from clean litter**. It is not an air filter, and its description should not be treated as confirmation of C32 compatibility.

![Official Robotail product image of the C42 small-hole filter.](/images/products/c42-filter.jpg)

*Official manufacturer product image.*

This distinction matters when searching for a replacement. “Filter” can describe different functions, so compare the intended job as well as the model name. A similar-looking component is not sufficient evidence that it is the right part.

If you are unsure which component you need, save the listing and ask the brand whether its described function matches your request.

## Keep a short checkout checklist

Before placing your order, gather these details in one place:

- **Your model:** Keep the cropped label photo available, with the complete identifier visible.
- **Your intended part:** Save the product link and the listing details you used to make your choice.
- **The matching evidence:** Compare the stated model and part function, rather than relying on silhouette.
- **Your destination:** For a US or European delivery, check shipping availability to your chosen region at checkout.
- **Any unresolved question:** Ask Robotail before accepting an ambiguous substitution.

A useful support message can be simple: “My model label reads [exact identifier]. I’m considering [product link] for [intended function]. Can you confirm this is the correct accessory?”

Once those details line up, you can place your order with a clearer understanding of what you are buying for your cat’s setup.
