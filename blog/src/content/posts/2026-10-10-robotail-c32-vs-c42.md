Choosing an automatic litter box is easier when you begin with the room and the cat who will use it. A feature list can tell you what a manufacturer offers, but it cannot tell you whether the entrance works for your cat or whether you can empty the waste compartment comfortably in your chosen corner.

Robotail's C32 and C42 offer two different design starting points. This guide compares the descriptions on the official product pages and adds a household checklist to help you decide what to investigate next. It is not a hands-on performance review.

## The main difference: the design

The [official C32 page](https://robotailpets.com/products/c32) describes an open-top box with a dual-rake cleaning design. The [official C42 page](https://robotailpets.com/products/c42) describes a roller-based box with a semi-open entrance and lists a 106 L capacity.

## Begin with your cat's existing habits

Look at the box your cat currently uses and note what you would want to preserve. Is the approach open? How does your cat enter and turn? Is there anything about the current location that already works well?

Those observations give you useful questions to take to the product instructions. If a fully open layout is your priority, the C32 is the natural model to research first. If the C42's semi-open, roller-based layout interests you, investigate its entry and interior arrangement before treating the capacity number as the deciding factor.

Neither is a universal recommendation. A model name alone cannot establish whether a particular cat will be comfortable using it.

## Measure space for use and upkeep

Mark out the proposed location before placing an order. In addition to the equipment footprint, consider the approach route and the space you will need to remove waste, add litter, and clean components.

Our shopping suggestion is to make a short list of unanswered questions: What are the current dimensions? Which components need removal for cleaning? What operating clearance does the manual require? Which litter is supported?

Find the answers for the exact model, not a similar-looking product. A larger advertised interior does not automatically mean a better fit for a smaller room, and a box that fits beneath furniture may still be awkward to maintain.

## Check the listing, not just the brand name

The listings above also provide a route to the [Robotail Store on Amazon US](https://www.amazon.com/stores/SafeSmartRobotail/page/02F4D9D4-25BA-42B5-B8AC-524E49F49D3D). Robotail products are offered through multiple sales listings, so start with the product and model you need rather than assuming one storefront contains every option.

Before buying on any channel, confirm the exact model, selected variant, included accessories, seller, and terms shown for your location. Do not transfer specifications or purchase conditions from one listing to another just because both use the Robotail name. Readers in Europe should check the listing serving their own country rather than assuming the US store has the same offer.

## Choose the questions you need answered

For the C32, begin with the open-top layout and how the rake-based design would fit your maintenance routine. For the C42, begin with the semi-open entrance, roller arrangement, and the space required in your home.

Then compare both against the same practical needs: your cat's access, your room layout, compatible litter, and everyday upkeep. That approach will give you a more useful shortlist than choosing the model with the longest feature list.

A welcoming home with cats is built around details you can live with every day. The right next step is the model whose instructions and design answer your household's questions—not a promise that one box is best for everyone.
